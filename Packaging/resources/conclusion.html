Wisp is in your menu bar

Look for the waveform icon at the top of the screen — there is no Dock icon. The welcome window will walk you through permissions and the one-time model download.

Once it says ready, hold Right ⌥ anywhere, talk, and let go.

Tip: select some text first, then hold the key and say what to change — “make this shorter”, “turn this into bullets” — and Wisp rewrites the selection instead of dictating.
