Dictation that stays on your Mac

Hold a key, say the thing, let go. The finished text appears wherever your cursor already is — cleaned up and punctuated.

Wisp will be installed into your Applications folder and opened for you. On first launch it asks for two permissions and downloads its speech model:

  • Microphone — to hear you while the key is held.
  • Accessibility — to see your hotkey and type into other apps.
  • Speech model — about 600 MB, downloaded once, then it works offline.

Speech is recognised on the Neural Engine and cleaned up by Apple Intelligence. There is no account and no server — nothing you say is uploaded.
